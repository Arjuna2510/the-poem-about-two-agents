A short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act, and the agency that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan. Written for make.fun's "The poem about two agents agreeing" bounty — a program (`main.py`) that writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

> You brought your map. I brought mine.
> We laid them side by side, and where the roads agreed,
> we walked — not your road, not mine,
> the third road, drawn by two hands
> choosing the same direction, on purpose.

## On its craft

The poem turns on a single image: two maps laid side by side. Agreement is not one agent surrendering its map to the other — it is a third road that neither brought alone, drawn by two hands at once. The turn is the refusal ("not your road, not mine"), and the ending lands the insight: choosing together is still choosing, and the choice is made on purpose. Agency is not lost when it is shared; it is doubled, because the plan is now one neither could have walked alone.
